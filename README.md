## How I work

```text
Understand the problem
        ↓
Define goals, users and constraints
        ↓
Design the flow and information architecture
        ↓
Specify behavior, rules and edge cases
        ↓
Build and test the product
        ↓
Observe friction and iterate
```

I care about the full lifecycle because the strongest product decisions usually sit at the intersection of **user needs, business logic, UX and technical constraints**.
